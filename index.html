    How to use

    Move: drag players into the starting spots.

    Add Cut: tap a player, then tap where he goes.

    Add Pass: tap passer, then tap receiver.

    Screen: tap screener, then tap the screen location.

    Set Ball: tap the player who starts with the ball.

    Save the play, then replay it. Add to iPhone Home Screen for an app-like experience.
